🎈 Pop Defense sprite uploader

Use this once to put your sprite images into the sprites/ folder of your repo. The game loads them automatically. Your normal deploy.html never touches that folder, so you can keep redeploying the game and the sprites stay. Anything you leave empty keeps the built-in drawn art.

1. Pick your images

No images chosen yet. PNG, GIF, WebP, JPG and SVG all work. Big files are shrunk automatically before upload.

2. Match images to towers

"Faces" is the way the character looks in your image, so it turns correctly to aim. Use "No turning" for buildings like the farm.

3. Match images to balloons

4. Upload

After uploading, GitHub Pages takes about a minute to update. Then refresh the game.
